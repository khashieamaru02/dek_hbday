DELGERMURUN — Birthday Website

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
3. Internet is needed for the Google Fonts and cinematic background photos.

HOW TO ADD YOUR FRIEND'S PHOTO
1. Put your chosen photo inside the "assets" folder.
2. Rename the photo exactly to: delgermurun.jpg
3. Refresh index.html. The photo will appear in the portrait section.

You can also replace the birthday message directly in index.html.
The website is responsive for desktop and mobile.
